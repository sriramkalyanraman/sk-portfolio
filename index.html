07 / FAQ

A few things you may want to know.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly content is available. A straightforward site can move from discovery to launch efficiently once the direction is agreed.

Do I need to provide the content?

You can provide your own content, or we can structure and refine the content requirements together as part of the project.

What about domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting can be arranged from €100/year, with the exact setup depending on your requirements.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Ongoing support is available, so you are not left to figure everything out once the website goes live.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. The project is agreed upfront and ongoing support is optional.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Redesigns are available where the existing site needs a clearer structure, stronger visual presentation or better mobile experience.
